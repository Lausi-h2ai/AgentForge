# Code of Conduct

This project expects respectful, professional collaboration.

## Our standards

- Be constructive and specific.
- Assume good intent, but be direct about technical issues.
- Keep criticism focused on code, design, and process.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and discussions
that violate these standards.

## Reporting

Report serious conduct issues through a private maintainer channel associated
with the repository.
