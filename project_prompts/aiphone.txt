Legal/Trademark Considerations for Mimicking Personalities
⚠️ Important constraint:

You mentioned "mimicking known personalities from the tech world" - this needs careful handling:

❌ Cannot do:

Clone actual voices without permission (Linus Torvalds' voice, etc.)

Use trademarked names in marketing ("Interview like Elon Musk!")

Impersonate real people

✅ Can do:

Personality types ("Direct and challenging like BDFL-style tech leaders")

Interview styles ("Socratic method questioner", "Deep technical diver")

Generic archetypes ("The Skeptical Architect", "The Pragmatic Engineer")

User can describe personality in text, AI adopts that style (no named person)

Safe approach:

"Choose your interviewer style: Encouraging Mentor, Neutral Professional, Challenging Critic, Detail-Oriented Architect"

In description: "Inspired by interview styles at top tech companies"
